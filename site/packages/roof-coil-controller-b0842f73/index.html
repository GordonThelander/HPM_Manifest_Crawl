Roof Coil Controller

A smart app written to turn on/off a switch between two date ranges, and temperature ranges. I use it to turn on roof coil switch between dec-apr when temp. is between 30-40, but settings are customizable. Used to prevent ice dams if I forget to turn heater switch on
