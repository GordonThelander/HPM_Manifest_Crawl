Levoit Air Purifiers, Humidifiers, and Fans

Hubitat drivers for Levoit smart devices via VeSync cloud API. Supports air purifiers (Core 200S/300S/400S/600S, Vital 100S/200S, Sprout Air, EverestAir), humidifiers (Classic 200S/300S, Superior 6000S, OasisMist 450S/1000S, LV600S, LV600S Hub Connect, Dual 200S, Sprout), and fans (Tower, Pedestal). EU region support.
