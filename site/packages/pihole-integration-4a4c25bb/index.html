Pihole Integration

Monitor and control one or more Pihole (Pi-hole) v6 DNS ad blockers from Hubitat. Exposes query, client, blocklist and host-health statistics, turns ad blocking on and off with an optional auto-expiring timer, and can drive every Pihole at once from a single group device.
