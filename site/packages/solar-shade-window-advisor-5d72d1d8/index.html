Solar Shade & Window Advisor

Advises when to draw shades and open/close windows per building side. Combines sun-path solar load, per-side occlusion (shared walls, partial walls, and computed shading from nearby structures), free Open-Meteo weather, and an adaptive thermal model that learns from your indoor temperature sensors to keep the house within a comfortable band around the thermostat setpoints.
