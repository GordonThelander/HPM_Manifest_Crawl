Navien NaviLink (Cloud)

Links a Navien tankless water heater or combi boiler to Hubitat through the NaviLink cloud service: power, hot water setpoint, on-demand recirculation, inlet and outlet temperature, flow rate and gas usage. No local wiring or bridge required. By jlslate.
